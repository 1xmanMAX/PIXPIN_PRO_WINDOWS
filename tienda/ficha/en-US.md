## Description

```text
PinDesk is a fast, private workspace for Windows: capture anything on your screen, keep it floating on top of your other windows, and organize notes, files and recordings in projects that work like chats.

It is built on native Windows technologies (Direct2D, DirectWrite, Media Foundation), so it starts instantly and runs smoothly even on modest PCs. No account is needed and there is no PinDesk cloud: your projects stay on your PC.

CAPTURE AND PIN
• Capture a region, a window, the whole screen or a scrolling page, record a GIF, or copy text with Windows OCR.
• Annotate right away with pen, arrows, boxes, text and pixelate.
• Pin images, videos, PDFs, notes and task lists so they stay on top of every window, or create a live pin that mirrors part of the screen in real time.

PROJECTS LIKE CHATS
• Every project is a chat-style notebook: notes, photos with captions, voice notes, documents, tables and canvases together.
• Read PDF, Word, EPUB, Markdown and HTML inside the app, with ink and bookmarks. Open Word, Excel and PowerPoint files without Office.
• Search everything, including the text inside your screenshots.

CANVAS AND TECHNICAL VIEWERS
• An infinite canvas with shapes, ink, dimensions, graphs, tables and exact constructions. Export to PNG, SVG or PDF.
• View AutoCAD DWG drawings, Revit and IFC 3D models and Civil 3D LandXML surfaces without the original software.

RECORD CLASSES AND MEETINGS
• One recorder for audio, screen video, webcam photos and clips, and screenshots placed at the right minute.
• Speech is transcribed on your PC. With an optional TypeSafe key, the Jev model ticks off your agenda topics live while you talk.
• "Study the class" shows the video, the synchronized transcript, the screenshots and your bookmarks side by side. Turn notes and recordings into Anki flashcards.
• Recordings are protected against crashes and power cuts.

TASKS AND SCHEDULE
• Type tasks in plain language ("#home call the bank 10/18 5:00 pm") and see them as a list or a Kanban board.
• A class schedule with reminders and an optional read-only Google Calendar view.
• A timeline of your day and a log of lessons learned.

AI ASSISTANT (OPTIONAL)
• An assistant chat that reads your documents, creates tasks and notes, draws on the canvas and can look things up on the web.
• It uses Anthropic Claude or DeepSeek with your own API key. Without a key, everything else works the same.

SYNC WITH YOUR PHONE
• Sync projects with the companion Android app over your local Wi-Fi, encrypted, without any cloud.
• Use your phone as an extra camera during a recording: its photos land at the exact minute.

Interface available in 14 languages. No ads and no telemetry.
```
